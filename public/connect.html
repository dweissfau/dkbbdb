dkbbdb

Three steps, about a minute. You need a computer with Chrome for this part; after that, any phone works.

1Install the extension

DraftKings only shows your drafts to your own signed-in browser, so a small Chrome extension reads them for you. It never sees your DraftKings password.

Checking for the extension…

2Connect it to your account

This links the extension to the account you are signed in with here.

3Sync your teams

Open your DraftKings contests page while signed in to DraftKings, then click Sync to dkbbdb in the panel at the top right. Re-sync whenever you draft new teams, and once after each playoff round when teams move to new leagues.
