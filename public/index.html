Add your DraftKings best ball teams

Two steps, about a minute, no sign-up. You need a computer with Chrome for this part; after that, any phone works.

1Install the extension

DraftKings only shows your drafts to your own signed-in browser, so a small Chrome extension reads them for you. It never sees your DraftKings password, and it only runs on your DraftKings contests page.

The extension is in private beta — ask for the install link.

2Sync your teams

Open your DraftKings contests page while signed in to DraftKings, then click Sync to dkbbdb in the panel at the top right. When it finishes it links straight to your page.

Open DraftKings contests ↗

Sync again whenever you draft new teams, and once after each playoff round when teams move to new leagues. Scores update on their own.

What you get

One page for your account: every season-long team's league place, cut line and points, and your exposure to every player — scored live from public NFL stats. There is no search on the site; your page is at dkbbdb.com/u/your-username and anyone you give that link to can see it.
