Privacy

dkbbdb is a free, live-scored view of DraftKings best ball teams. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page says exactly what it stores and how to have it removed.

What the extension reads

The extension runs only on your DraftKings contests page, and only when you click Sync to dkbbdb. It reads your own paid, season-long best ball entries and the draft board of each one (the 12 usernames in the league and who drafted whom), using the DraftKings session already open in your browser.

For each tournament you are in, it also reads the tournament's payout table (the guaranteed prize for reaching each round) from the tournament's own DraftKings pages, sending only the payout-related parts. If you open a tournament's page on DraftKings, the extension reads the payout table shown there the same way. That table is stored once per tournament and shared by everyone in it.

It never reads or sends your DraftKings password, cookies, email, balance, payment details, or any contest that is not best ball.

What dkbbdb stores

For each synced team: the contest name, buy-in and size, the draft date, the DraftKings usernames in that league, every team's drafted players, and any prize DraftKings reports for your entry. Scores are not taken from DraftKings: they are worked out from public NFL statistics.

For every upload, a log line: the time, the DraftKings usernames synced, how many teams, and a scrambled code standing in for the sender's internet address (used to stop abuse). The address itself is never stored.

What is public

Everything you sync. Your page is at dkbbdb.com/u/your-username and anyone who has that address can open it; your teams also appear, under your DraftKings username, on the site's leaderboard for each tournament (dkbbdb.com/leaderboard). The opponents in your leagues appear inside the league view, as they do on DraftKings, and have no page of their own.

What is never collected

No names, emails, passwords, payment information, location, browsing history or advertising identifiers. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party trackers. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing your teams

Email us with your DraftKings username and your teams are deleted, usually within a few days. Uninstalling the extension stops any further syncing immediately.
